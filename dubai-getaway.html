Your 5-Night Itinerary

A carefully crafted day-by-day plan so you know exactly what to expect — relaxing, exciting, and stress-free from start to finish.

Day 1 · Arrival

Lagos → Dubai: Arrival & Check-In

Depart Lagos (LOS) — overnight or early morning flight to Dubai
Arrive Dubai International Airport (DXB) — meet your private transfer driver
Check in to your 5-star hotel and freshen up
Evening welcome dinner at the hotel or a nearby restaurant
Rest and settle in — your adventure starts tomorrow
Day 2 · Sightseeing

Dubai City Tour — Icons & Landmarks

Breakfast at hotel
Guided visit to the Burj Khalifa (At the Top observation deck) with breathtaking 360° views
Explore the massive Dubai Mall — the world's largest shopping centre
Walk across the iconic Dubai Frame — a 150m picture frame over the city
Free evening — JBR Beach walk or The Dubai Fountain water show
Day 3 · Adventure

Desert Safari — Dunes, Camels & BBQ

Morning at leisure — hotel pool, spa, or personal exploration
Afternoon pickup for your Evening Desert Safari
Thrilling dune bashing in 4x4 SUVs across the golden sand dunes
Camel riding, sandboarding, and traditional Arabic photo opportunities
Sunset BBQ dinner in a Bedouin camp with live entertainment under the stars
Day 4 · Culture & Shopping

Old Dubai, Gold Souk & Free Time

Breakfast at hotel
Morning visit to the historic Gold Souk — thousands of jewellery pieces on display
Explore the fragrant Spice Souk and the vibrant Al Fahidi Heritage District
Traditional Abra (water taxi) ride across Dubai Creek
Afternoon and evening free — last-minute shopping at Mall of the Emirates or JBR Beach
Day 5 · Departure

Check-Out & Return to Lagos

Final breakfast at hotel
Optional morning activity — brunch cruise or last shopping run
Hotel check-out and private transfer to Dubai Airport (DXB)
Board return flight to Lagos (LOS)
Arrive home — rested, inspired, and full of stories 🧡
